Personal Development - Twelve Pillars Reflection

Chapter 4: Personal Development

Summary: In this chapter of The Twelve Pillars, Jim Rohn teaches that success begins with personal development. He emphasizes that income and achievement rarely exceed personal growth. If we want our lives to improve, we must improve ourselves first. Growth is intentional. It requires discipline, learning, self-reflection, and the courage to change. Success is not something we chase; it is something we attract by becoming better internally.

Personal Application: Personal development for me has never been optional it has been survival. I was diagnosed with cerebral palsy diplegia at a tender age. My mother carried me on her back to school when I could not walk long distances. I endured surgeries that were meant to correct my condition but instead left my ligaments weaker. I spent almost a year in a coma. When I woke up, I did not wake up to a “normal” childhood I woke up to a wheelchair.

There were moments I could have given up. Moments when discrimination, financial hardship, and physical pain felt heavier than hope. But I remembered Romans 8:28 — “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God.” I realized that my growth would not come from comfort but from pressure. Every surgery, every setback, every delay became a tool shaping my character. I returned to school after missing months due to hospital stays. I went back to finish school. I pursued ICT certifications among others and continued my BSc in Computer Network & Security. My body may have limitations, but my mind and spirit refuse to be limited. Through it all, I am becoming stronger, wiser, and more resilient. My development is my victory.

Chapter 5: Goal Setting

Summary: This chapter highlights the importance of writing down goals. Goals give direction and purpose. Without goals, life becomes reactive instead of intentional. The authors explain that goals are powerful not just because of what we achieve, but because of who we become in the process of achieving them.

Personal Application: I remember one rainy day clearly. I was on my way to Mathematics class, struggling to open the gate while rain poured down on me. Someone stood nearby laughing, saying that I would never get an “A.” In that moment, soaked and humiliated, I looked up and spoke to God. I declared that this would be the last time I returned to that class I would pass.

That moment was not just about passing a subject; it was about reclaiming my confidence. I held on to Philippians 4:13 “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.” And I did pass. That victory taught me that goals must be declared boldly and pursued relentlessly. My goals now are bigger: completing my degree at the University of Technology, Jamaica, becoming financially independent, and creating opportunities for myself and others with disabilities. Even when financial aid was denied and accommodations were uncertain, I remembered Jeremiah 29:11 “For I know the plans I have for you… plans to prosper you and not to harm you.” My goals are rooted not just in ambition but in faith.

Chapter 6: The Importance of the Right Associations

Summary: This chapter teaches that the people we surround ourselves with shape our future. Associations influence mindset, confidence, and direction. To grow, we must align ourselves with those who inspire and challenge us.

Personal Application: I have experienced both neglect and loyalty. My father wasn’t around because he believed he could not handle raising a child with a disability. But my mother stayed. She carried me. She pushed my wheelchair. She went up and down hills to make sure I received an education. Her strength became my example of unconditional love and sacrifice.

Along my journey, I faced people who doubted my abilities. Some employers saw my walker before they saw my qualifications. Yet God placed mentors, programs, and opportunities in my path TEF, USF, HEART/NSTA Trust, Caribbean Maritime University amon others, and now UTech. These associations strengthened me. I hold onto Proverbs 27:17 “Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.” I am learning to surround myself with people who sharpen, not weaken, my purpose.

Chapter 7: The Power of Attitude

Summary: This chapter explains that attitude determines success. We cannot control every circumstance, but we can control our response. A positive attitude transforms obstacles into opportunities.

Personal Application: There were nights I cried silently. Nights when financial pressure felt unbearable. Nights when I questioned why my journey had to be so difficult. Yet each time, I found strength in Isaiah 40:31 — “But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles.”

I learned that my disability does not define my destiny. My attitude does. I chose faith over bitterness. I chose perseverance over self-pity. I chose to believe that my story is not one of limitation but of divine purpose. Even now, facing accommodation struggles and student loan repayment uncertainty, I remind myself that God did not bring me this far to leave me. My life is not easy, but it is meaningful. And I will continue walking slowly if I must, but forward always.
