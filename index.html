Gay Ann Clarke

Computer Networking & Security (Cybersecurity) University of Technology, Jamaica

Hi, my name is Gay Ann Clarke. I am 28 years old, born on October 2, 1997, in the parish of Manchester, Jamaica.

I am the youngest of five girls from my mother and second born of twins, which has played a significant role in shaping who I am today.

I spent most of my life in Chapelton, where my mother is from. I attended primary school and later Clarendon College, where I completed my high school education. My journey through school was not without its challenges. As a person with a physical disability, I experienced a number of obstacles along the way. However, these experiences strengthened my resilience, determination, and commitment to achieving my goals.

After completing high school, I matriculated to the Caribbean Maritime University in conjunction with HEART/NSTA Trust, where I successfully completed studies in ICT and Digitalization. Building on that foundation, I am currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Networking and Security, majoring in Cybersecurity, at the University of Technology, Jamaica, where I continue to develop my knowledge and skills in the field of technology.
