My Hero & AI Business Report

My Hero: My Mother

"I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." – Philippians 4:13

Background: My mother is the strongest person I know. From the moment I was diagnosed with cerebral palsy diplegia, she made my life her mission. She carried me to school when I could not walk long distances. She pushed my wheelchair up and down hills. She sat beside hospital beds through surgeries, therapy sessions, and even during the year I was in a coma. When others doubted my future, she never did.

Why She Is My Hero: My mother embodies resilience, sacrifice, and unconditional love. When my father walked away because he felt he could not manage raising a child with a disability, she stayed. She fought for my education. She encouraged me when I felt discouraged. She believed in my ability even when my body felt weak and society saw limitations. Because of her strength, I learned perseverance. Because of her faith, I developed courage. Because of her sacrifices, I am pursuing my degree in Computer Network & Cyber Security today. That scripture — Philippians 4:13 — is not just a verse to me it is a declaration over my life. It represents the mindset she helped instill in me: with faith, discipline, and determination, nothing is impossible.

AI Business Report 2026

My Chosen AI Business Idea: Personalized Learning AI Tutors

Business Concept: The Personalized Learning AI Tutor is an intelligent system designed to provide customized academic support to students based on their learning style, pace, strengths, and weaknesses. Using artificial intelligence, the platform would analyze performance data and adapt lesson delivery in real time. This would benefit students who struggle in traditional classroom settings or require additional reinforcement.

Application in My World: As a Computer Network & Cyber Security student, I understand how technology can solve real-world problems. Many students face barriers such as disabilities, financial hardship, or limited access to support. This AI solution could create inclusive educational opportunities and support students who need flexible learning environments.

Ethical Concerns:

  • Privacy and protection of student data
  • Potential job displacement of educators
  • Bias in AI algorithms
  • Over-reliance on technology

Proposed Solutions:

  • Implement strong cybersecurity and encryption protocols
  • Ensure compliance with data protection regulations
  • Design AI systems to assist teachers rather than replace them
  • Conduct regular audits to reduce algorithmic bias
